✨ Premium Features

Free for Everyone

Direct messaging is free for all users. Connect, chat, share posts — no paywall on basic communication.

📸 Stories Share photo and video stories that last 1-3 days. Choose your duration. Free for all users.

Premium Features ($10.99/month)

Become a creator. Set your own prices. Keep 100% of what you earn.

🔒 Lock Your Content Set your own price. Lock your entire profile or individual posts. Free users see a preview; paying fans unlock the full content. You choose the price - $1, $5, $20, whatever fits your content.
⭐ Premium Badge Gold badge next to your name everywhere. Signals you're a creator and your content is worth paying for.
📹 Video Chat One-on-one encrypted video calls with other premium users. Free users can receive calls but only premium can initiate.
📸 Camera-Only DMs Send real-time camera snaps and video messages in chat - no gallery uploads, authentic only. Premium exclusive.
🔴 Go Live Stream live to your connects with real-time chat and viewer counts. Premium only. Lock your livestream behind your paywall if you want.

How You Earn

💰 You Set the Price Not a fixed price. You choose what your content is worth. $2 for a photo set, $10 for a video, $50 for a livestream — whatever. You keep 100% after PayPal fees. The platform takes nothing.
🎯 Direct Payout Payments go straight to your PayPal. No monthly threshold. No waiting period. Instant when someone unlocks your content.

Why Premium?

Go Premium to unlock creator tools — live streaming, subscriber-only posts, and direct fan support. You set your own prices and keep what you earn. No middleman. Just you and your audience.
